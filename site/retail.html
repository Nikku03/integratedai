03 — Shops

Retail you walk slower in.

Flagships, concept stores, boutiques and roll-out formats. We design shops that stop people on the pavement, pull them to the back wall, and make the product the hero.

Our approach

Fixtures that disappear.

The best retail interiors are almost invisible: the eye goes to the product, the feet go where you want them, and the till is exactly where someone reaches for their card.

  • — Shopfront and window strategy: what stops someone at 4 km/h
  • — Floor plans built on decompression zones, sightlines and the "power wall"
  • — Modular fixture systems you can re-merchandise every season
  • — Lighting that renders colour truthfully — no surprises at home
  • — Roll-out kits of parts for multi-site brands, with a local twist per city
