What a shop needs

Designed for the three seconds at the window.

Four things we settle before anyone chooses a finish.

  1. 01

    Three seconds at the window

    Someone walking past at 4 km/h gives a shopfront about three seconds. We design the window for those, then the walk to the back wall, then the till.

  2. 02

    Fixtures that disappear

    Rails, plinths and shelves in the colours of the room, so the stock is the only thing anyone looks at.

  3. 03

    Fitting rooms people like

    Room to turn round, a curtain to the floor, a hook for a bag and light that renders colour truthfully.

  4. 04

    A kit that travels

    Every fixture drawn as a kit of parts and made in our workshop, ready for the next store in the next city.

    Week 3 · Partitions and making good

    Fitting-room walls framed and boarded, the arch re-plastered, the ceiling made good for the new panels.

  4. 04 Joinery & fit-out

    Weeks 5–7 · Joinery and fixtures

    The brass rail, the mirror and the curtain tracks go up. Planters and a chair are set out round them.

  5. 05 Finishes

    Week 10 · Lighting

    Doors at eleven. Everything on the rail is within reach, and every fitting room has a mirror.

Questions

What owners ask us first.

More questions
Will you work in my city?

Very likely. We work across India, and abroad with our own site lead and a local contractor under our contract. Recent projects include Lisbon and Dubai.

Placeholder

Will I know what it looks like before it’s built?

Yes. You walk through it in 3D, handle every finish on a sample board, and for the pieces that matter most — the counter, a banquette, the bar top — we build a full-size section before we make the rest.

Do you handle licences and permits?

We draw what your landlord and the authorities need — fire and exit layouts, extraction routes, signage permissions, mall fit-out submissions — and design to those conditions from day one, so nothing is ripped out later. The licences themselves (in India: food, trade, excise, fire NOC) are held in your name. We work alongside your licensing consultant, or introduce one we trust.
