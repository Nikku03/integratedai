Ember

A wood-fire restaurant in a former Alfama warehouse. 84 covers around an open hearth, built to feel like it's always been there.

LocationAlfama, Lisbon
TypeRestaurant · wood-fire
Size310 m² · 84 covers
OpenedMarch 2025
ScopeConcept → site
The brief

Put the fire in the middle.

The chef wanted every seat to see the hearth. The building wanted to keep its stone walls and awkward columns. We planned the room as three terraces stepping down toward the fire, so the columns became the edges of rooms rather than obstacles in one.

Materials were kept to four: lime plaster, blackened steel, oak and a hand-glazed terracotta tile that runs from the bar front to the kitchen pass. Lighting is warm and low, with a single scene change at dusk.
