How it works

Five steps. One team.

The same senior lead runs every step, so nothing gets lost between the drawing and the building.

01/ 05Walk

  1. Walk

    We see the site, ideally at the hour it has to work. Survey, services, capacity, red flags. We learn your menu and your service before we pick up a pen.

    You get
    A feasibility note, a budget range and a programme
    Typically
    1–2 weeks

  2. Design

    Plan first, then the look. Layout options, concept, palette and a 3D walk-through. The big moves are agreed here, so they don’t move later.

    You get
    A concept pack and an agreed layout
    Typically
    3–6 weeks

  3. Detail

    Joinery, lighting, finishes, FF&E and services drawn to the millimetre. Samples on site, a full-size mock-up of anything we can’t afford to get wrong, then the fixed price.

    You get
    A full drawing set and a fixed price on one contract
    Typically
    3–6 weeks

  4. Build

    Our site lead, our trades, one programme. Long-lead items are already on order. You get a weekly site walk and a daily diary, and no calls from the electrician.

    You get
    The room, on the date
    Typically
    6–20 weeks

  5. Open

    Snagging, dressing, a walk-through for your team, opening night. Ninety days later we come back to fix whatever service has found.

    You get
    A handover pack and the 90-day return visit
    Typically
    Opening week, then day 90

Questions

What owners ask us first.

It depends on three things: the size of the space, the state of the shell, and how much is made rather than bought. We’ll tell you on the first call whether your number is realistic, including when it isn’t. After the site visit you get a range; after detail design, a fixed price on one contract. Kitchen equipment, deposits and licence fees sit outside that number, and we’ll help you plan for them.

A 100–150 m² café usually takes 10–14 weeks from signed design to opening: 4–6 weeks of drawings and approvals, then 6–8 weeks on site. Restaurants and bars with full kitchens and extraction take 16–24 weeks. The build is rarely the critical path. Approvals and long-lead items are: kitchen equipment, custom lighting, stone. We order those early.

We draw what your landlord and the authorities need — fire and exit layouts, extraction routes, signage permissions, mall fit-out submissions — and design to those conditions from day one, so nothing is ripped out later. The licences themselves (in India: food, trade, excise, fire NOC) are held in your name. We work alongside your licensing consultant, or introduce one we trust.
