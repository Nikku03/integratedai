Morning · Café

Light first, then everything else.

A café earns its living before ten. We plan the counter for a queue that moves, the seats for laptops and prams, and the light for pastries that photograph. Daylight does most of the work. We make sure it lands where it should.

Afternoon · Restaurant

The long lunch, held.

Warm walls, walnut floors, banquettes you sink into. By two o’clock the room should feel as if it has been busy for hours: full but calm, with every seat facing something worth looking at.

Night · Bar

The back-bar is the stage.

The light drops, the bottles glow, the counter fills. We design bars for this exact hour: sightlines to the back-bar, a top that takes a knock, and acoustics that let the music lift without anyone shouting.

Three rooms. One day. One team.
