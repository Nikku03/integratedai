The studio

A room is a sequence, not a picture.

Good hospitality interiors are choreographed: the first three seconds at the door, the view from the table, the light on the bar at 11pm, the width of the aisle when the shop is full. We design for those moments — then build them to survive a Saturday night.

We work with independent operators and groups alike, from single-site restaurants to roll-out retail formats. Every project is led by a senior designer from first sketch to snag list.

01
Arrival

The first three seconds

From the pavement to the host stand, a guest decides how they feel about the night. We design the threshold — the reveal, the light level drop, the first thing you see — before anything else.

02
The room

Every seat is a good seat

Banquettes for the long lunch, two-tops with a view of the bar, a communal table that feels like a choice rather than a consolation. We plan the room around sightlines, not just covers.

03
The detail

What people photograph

A brass rail worn smooth, a tiled back-bar, the way a pendant catches the glassware. The details are the marketing. We design them to be found, not announced.

How we work

Four stages, one team

01

Discover

Site visit, operator interviews, service flow, budget reality-check. We map how the room needs to work before we decide how it looks.

02

Concept

Narrative, palette, plan options and a 3D walk-through. This is where the brand becomes a place — and where we lock the big moves.

03

Detail

Joinery drawings, lighting design, FF&E schedules, finishes samples on site. Everything a contractor needs to price and build without guessing.

04

Deliver

Tender, contractor management, weekly site walks, snagging and dressing. We're there on opening night — usually near the bar.
