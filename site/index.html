The build

From the first line to opening night.

Scroll, and the dining room builds itself: the plan, the sketch, the shell, the joinery, the finishes, the lights. A dining room at two in the afternoon. One team from the first drawing to the first service.

The build: a restaurant shown in seven stages, from the first drawing to the first service, with a short note from the studio at each stage. At the end you can see our dining rooms or start a project. You can skip the build at any time.

Motion is reduced on your device, so the build is shown as three stills: the drawing, the shell and the finished room.

14:00 · Restaurant01 / 07Skip the build ↓

  1. 01 The drawing

    Week 1 · Measured survey

    An arcade down the middle, glass on two sides. Booths go on the blind wall; the tables take the light.

  2. 02 The sketch

    Week 2 · Concept

    Seen from under the arches, the way a guest first sees the room.

  3. 03 The shell

    Week 5 · Plaster and making good

    Ceiling roses restored, arches re-run in lime plaster, columns stripped back to the iron.

  4. 04 Joinery & fit-out

    Weeks 7–10 · Booths, tables, the station

    Booths on the blind wall. The service station sits behind the column, so the floor team never crosses the room.

  5. 05 Finishes

    Weeks 11–12 · Floors and panelling

    Boards sanded and oiled dark, walnut on the booth wall, green leather on the booths, the shopfront reglazed.

  6. 06 Lights on

    Week 13 · Lighting

    Opal globes at 2,700 K for the evening. By day the windows do the work.

  7. 07 Open

    First service · 12:00

    Full but calm by two, and every seat faces something worth looking at.
