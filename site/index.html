The studio

A room is a sequence, not a picture.

Good hospitality interiors are choreographed: the first three seconds at the door, the view from the table, the light on the bar at 11pm, the width of the aisle when the shop is full. We design for those moments — then build them ourselves, so nothing gets lost between the drawing and the site.

Design and build under one roof means one contract, one programme and one team that has to live with its own decisions. Every project is led by a senior designer from first sketch to snag list.

How we work

Four stages, one team

01

Discover

Site visit, operator interviews, service flow, budget reality-check. We map how the room needs to work before we decide how it looks.

02

Design

Narrative, palette, plan options and a 3D walk-through. This is where the brand becomes a place — and where we lock the big moves.

03

Detail

Joinery drawings, lighting design, FF&E schedules, finishes samples on site. Everything our own build team needs to price and build without guessing.

04

Build

Our team on site, weekly walks, snagging and dressing. One contract, one programme. We're there on opening night — usually near the bar.
