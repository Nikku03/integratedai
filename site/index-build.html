The build

From the first line to opening night.

Scroll, and each room builds itself: the plan, the sketch, the shell, the joinery, the finishes, the lights. A café at eight, a restaurant at two, a bar at eleven. One team from the first drawing to the first service.

The build: a café, a restaurant and a bar, each shown in seven stages from the first drawing to opening night. At the end of the café you can take the corner table to see our services. You can skip the build at any time.

Motion is reduced on your device, so each build is shown as three stills: the drawing, the shell and the finished room.

08:00 · Day one

Café

08:00 · Café01 / 07

  1. 01 The drawing

    Week 1 · Measured survey

    Counter on the brick wall, so the queue forms along it and not across the door. The stair stays where it is.

  2. 02 The sketch

    Week 2 · Concept

    Drawn from the door: the trusses, the steel stair, a ceiling of plants to walk in under.

  3. 03 The shell

    Week 4 · Strip-out and making good

    Brick cleaned back, trusses painted, the mezzanine made safe. Everything stays white until it’s decided.

  4. 04 Joinery & fit-out

    Weeks 6–9 · Joinery and FF&E

    The steel stair goes in first, then the counter and the bench. Planters next, and the tables work round them.

  5. 05 Finishes

    Weeks 10–11 · Floor, brick, paint

    Screed polished, brick sealed, walls in a warm white that holds the morning light. Beech tops, a pine counter.

  6. 06 Lights on

    Week 12 · Lighting and commissioning

    Bare lamps on long flex at 2,700 K. 300 lux on the counter, softer everywhere else.

  7. 07 Open

    Day one · 08:00

    Doors at eight. The queue moves.

14:00 · Restaurant01 / 07

  1. 01 The drawing

    Week 1 · Measured survey

    An arcade down the middle, glass on two sides. Booths go on the blind wall; the tables take the light.

  2. 02 The sketch

    Week 2 · Concept

    Seen from under the arches, the way a guest first sees the room.

  3. 03 The shell

    Week 5 · Plaster and making good

    Ceiling roses restored, arches re-run in lime plaster, columns stripped back to the iron.

  4. 04 Joinery & fit-out

    Weeks 7–10 · Booths, tables, the station

    Booths on the blind wall. The service station sits behind the column, so the floor team never crosses the room.

  5. 05 Finishes

    Weeks 11–12 · Floors and panelling

    Boards sanded and oiled dark, walnut on the booth wall, green leather on the booths, the shopfront reglazed.

  6. 06 Lights on

    Week 13 · Lighting

    Opal globes at 2,700 K for the evening. By day the windows do the work.

  7. 07 Open

    First service · 12:00

    Full but calm by two, and every seat faces something worth looking at.

23:00 · Opening night

Bar

See the bars →

23:00 · Bar01 / 07

  1. 01 The drawing

    Week 1 · Measured survey

    One long counter, the back-bar on the wall behind it, a speed rail at every station.

  2. 02 The sketch

    Week 2 · Concept

    Drawn from a stool. The back-bar is the stage; the counter is the front row.

  3. 03 The shell

    Week 4 · Carcass

    Counter framed in steel, the back-bar wall lined and levelled, the ceiling battened out.

  4. 04 Joinery & fit-out

    Weeks 6–8 · Back-bar and stools

    The back-bar shelves go up, three pendants are hung, and three stools are set at 750 mm.

  5. 05 Finishes

    Weeks 9–10 · Veneers and stone

    Walnut on the front and across the ceiling, a honed marble top that takes a knock, green velvet on the stools.

  6. 06 Lights on

    Week 11 · Scenes

    Three blown-glass pendants, a red wash over the corner, the back-bar at 40 lux. Nothing brighter than the bottles.

  7. 07 Open

    Opening night · 23:00

    The light drops, the bottles glow, the counter fills.
