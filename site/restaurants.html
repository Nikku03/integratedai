01 — Restaurants

Dining rooms with a pulse.

Fine dining, neighbourhood bistros, all-day cafés and 200-cover flagships. We design restaurants around the service — so the room works as hard as the kitchen.

Our approach

The plan is the menu.

Before a finish is chosen we walk the service: pass to table, table to loo, host to bar. Every metre a server saves is a metre of calm in the room.

  • — Cover plans tested against your target turns and average spend
  • — Acoustic strategy so a full room stays conversational
  • — Layered lighting: daylight, dinner, late — on scenes, not dimmers
  • — Kitchen and bar sightlines designed with your chef, not around them
  • — Finishes specified for spills, chairs and 10-year wear
