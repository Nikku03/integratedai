What a restaurant needs

Drawn around the service.

Four things we settle before anyone chooses a finish.

  1. 01

    The service path

    Host to table, table to pass, pass to wash-up. We walk it before we choose a finish: every metre a server saves is a metre of calm in the room.

  2. 02

    Acoustics at full covers

    Absorption in the ceiling, the banquette backs and under the tables. We test it with the room full, so a table for two can still talk at nine.

  3. 03

    Banquettes worth waiting for

    Made in our workshop, with a seat you can stay in for three courses. They seat more covers than loose chairs, and they’re the seats people ask for.

  4. 04

    Light for food and faces

    Warm and low over the tables, brighter at the pass, and set on scenes that dim through the evening without anyone touching a switch.

Questions

What owners ask us first.

More questions
What will it cost?

It depends on three things: the size of the space, the state of the shell, and how much is made rather than bought. We’ll tell you on the first call whether your number is realistic, including when it isn’t. After the site visit you get a range; after detail design, a fixed price on one contract. Kitchen equipment, deposits and licence fees sit outside that number, and we’ll help you plan for them.

Do you design the kitchen?

We plan the kitchen with your chef and a specialist kitchen consultant: the layout, the pass, the services and the extraction. We build the room around it and coordinate the equipment supplier. We don’t sell equipment, so we have no reason to over-specify it.

Who manages the contractors?

We do. On a design & build project every trade works for us, under one contract and one programme, with one of our site leads in charge. You get a weekly site walk and a daily diary. You don’t get calls from the electrician.
