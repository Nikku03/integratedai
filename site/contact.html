      What happens next

      Three steps, then a plan.

      1. 01

        We reply.

        Within two working days, from the person who would lead your project, with a few questions and a time to talk.

      2. 02

        We walk the site.

        Ideally at the hour it has to work. We bring a tape, a lux meter and a lot of questions about your menu.

      3. 03

        You get a plan and a range.

        A short proposal: how we’d approach the room, what to order from the menu, a realistic budget range, a programme and who leads it.
