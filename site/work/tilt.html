Espresso bar· Sector 44, Gurugram· 2024

An espresso bar in a working office lobby, built in 19 nights without closing the building.

Type
Espresso bar

Counter & workstation · Joinery · Lighting · Signage · Build
Duration
5 weeks design · 19 nights on site
Opened
September 2024

The brief

A roaster opening its first bar in an office lobby. Four hundred people pass between 08:30 and 09:30, the landlord allowed no daytime works, and there was no gas. Speed first, room second.

The move

The counter is the room.

A single 7.2-metre bar runs the length of the unit. You order at one end and collect at the other, so the queue and the pick-up never cross, and everything behind the counter is set out for two baristas at peak and one after lunch.

The details

01 / 03

  1. 01Workstation

    Grinders, knock-box chute and milk fridge all within one step of the group heads, drawn from the head barista’s reach.

  2. 02Counter

    A 30 mm solid-surface top with a routed drip channel; the front is vertical ash battens that lift out for access to the plumbing.

  3. 03Sign

    Backlit brushed brass, sized to read from the lift lobby 30 metres away.

The build

All joinery was made off-site and installed between 22:00 and 06:00, with the lobby cleaned and handed back every morning. Water, drainage and a 32-amp supply went in through the ceiling void over four nights, before the counter arrived.

The outcome

Peak hour runs on two baristas, and the queue stops short of the lift doors.

The landlord has since asked us to plan the building’s second food-and-drink unit.
