Café & bakery· Indiranagar, Bengaluru· 2025

Placeholder project. The name, quote and photographs stand in for real work.

An all-day café and bakery in a 1970s Indiranagar house, planned so the morning queue and the laptop crowd never meet.

Café & bakery

Opened
February 2025

The brief

A bakery with a cult following at the weekend market wanted a permanent home: a café that could pour 300 coffees before ten, then keep people until four. The house had a deep verandah, three small rooms and a staircase in exactly the wrong place.

The move

Two speeds, one house.

We opened the front rooms into a fast lane: door, counter, pick-up, out. The back rooms stayed small and slow, and between the two the bakery sits behind glass, so you queue past the lamination bench and sit where you can watch the ovens.

The details

01 / 03

  1. 01Counter

    The espresso machine sits in a section of counter dropped by 150 mm, so the barista faces the queue, not the back of a machine.

  2. 02Light

    Daylight carries the morning. From 16:00 the scene steps down to 2,700 K, with the pastry case held at 500 lux and the tables at 150.

  3. 03Joinery

    Window benches in teak reclaimed from the house’s original doors, with a charging point under every second cushion.

The build

We built in two phases so the bakery could be commissioned and test-baked for three weeks before the café opened. On a residential street, the neighbours had the programme and our site lead’s number before the first delivery.

The outcome

The queue runs out of the door instead of through the room, and the back rooms stay full well into the afternoon.

The owners added a second daily bake within two months of opening.
