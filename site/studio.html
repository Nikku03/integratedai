The studio

We design the rooms where evenings happen.

Atelier Nord is a small studio with a long snag list. We've opened restaurants, bars and shops in nine cities since 2016 — always with the same people from first sketch to first service.

What we believe

Three rules

01

Design the service, then the room

A beautiful room that slows the kitchen is a bad room. We start with the plan of how people work in the space and let the interior follow.

02

Build for year five

Hospitality interiors take a beating. We specify for spills, chairs, heels and hangovers, so the room looks better with wear — not tired.

03

One senior lead, start to finish

No hand-offs to a junior after concept. The designer who sold you the idea is the one on site at 7am checking the tile setting-out.

People Twelve of us, plus the contractors we keep coming back to
