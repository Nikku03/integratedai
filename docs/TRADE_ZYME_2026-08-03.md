Decided 3 Aug 2026, market open, ZYME $21.84.

This is a judgment call and it is labelled as one. The model contributed three
mechanical things — a dated catalyst calendar pulled from filing text, a
dilution-readiness screen over the filing index, and the loser-avoidance
discipline that survived testing. It contributed **no** view on direction,
because every direction test in this project failed. The probability estimates
below are mine.

The two disqualifications worth spelling out:

**REPL is the trade most people would take, and I am not taking it.** It has the
cleanest story — FDA briefing documents crushed it 38% on 28 Jul, it was halted
all of 30 Jul, and the panel voted **10-3 in favour against its own reviewers**,
reopening at $11.38 from a $5.41 halt. But its S-3/A went **effective at 04:15
ET on 30 Jul, AdCom morning**, three days before its action date. Registration
takes weeks; it cannot be done after the news. That is a filed, under-oath
statement of intent to sell stock into the outcome. An approval is met with an
offering at a discount within days. Against a ~30% chance of a second CRL on a
name with ~7.5 months of cash, the asymmetry runs backwards.

**CAPR** goes to a PDUFA on 22 Aug having lost its advisory committee 3-for /
9-against, with Q1 burn annualising to $120m against $105m of cash. The positive
Lancet HOPE-3 publication landed while the stock was halted and changed nothing.

## Why the −18% drift is the opportunity, not the warning

ZYME fell from $26.72 on 7 Jul to $21.84 across twelve sessions. There is **no
down day of 5% or more since 8 May** — it is a grind, on *falling* volume, 0.18m
shares today against a 0.4m norm.

A market pricing a rejection trades heavy. This one has gone quiet. The
explanation is in the 29 Jun 8-K: ZYME agreed to acquire Theravance Biopharma,
financed by a $350m non-recourse note secured solely by US YUPELRI royalties
plus ~$360m of Theravance net cash at closing. Zymeworks is converting itself
from a biotech story into a royalty aggregator, and the generalist biotech
holders have been quietly rotating out of the story change. That is a
shareholder-base rotation, not a verdict on the FDA.

Meanwhile the company authorised a **$125m buyback on 14 May** — 7.8% of its own
market cap — and has filed no S-3, no 424B5 and no ATM in 120 days. Management is
buying what the rotating holders are selling.

## The numbers I am actually betting on

An sBLA is a label expansion of a drug the FDA **already approved**. Manufacturing
and safety are established; the open question is the trial. Priority Review means
FDA judged it a significant improvement over available therapy, and no advisory
committee was called. I put approval at **85%** — my estimate, not a measured
base rate on this dataset.

| Approval | 85% | +45% | +38.3% |

The downside is shallow because $244m of cash is 15% of the cap, the buyback is

For contrast, REPL on the same arithmetic: 70% × +28% + 30% × −58% = **+2.2%**.

## Exit rule — and where I override the model

The pre-registered rule from this project is arm at +8%, then trail 8%. **Do not
use it here.** It would have stopped out repeatedly during exactly the
twelve-session drift that created the entry. For a dated binary the price does
not travel a path to the outcome — it jumps.

1. **No pre-event stop.** Hold to 25 Aug or do not put the trade on.
2. **On approval:** take the gap. Sell into the first session's strength, or arm
   the 8% trail from the post-gap high. The $250m is a one-time payment; the
   royalty re-rating is a multi-quarter thesis and a different trade. No greed.
3. **On CRL:** exit at the open. Do not average down.
4. **Sizing is the only risk control that works**, because the move is a gap and
   no stop can be filled inside it. A 15% chance of −25% carries a full slot;
   REPL's 30% chance of −58% could not have.

## What I am not claiming

This is not a moonshot and I will not call it one. The partnered economics cap
it: Jazz sells the drug, ZYME collects a defined $250m and a royalty. **The
reason it is confident is the same reason it is capped.** A true 100%+ shot
requires accepting coin-flip odds, and dressing a coin flip up as conviction is
the one thing this project has spent months learning not to do.

The honest ceiling here is roughly +45%, the honest floor roughly −25%, and the
edge is that the market is not currently paying for either.

Standing caveats: the historical panel is 100% survivors, so every backward
return in this repo is optimistic; and the 85% is judgment, not measurement.
